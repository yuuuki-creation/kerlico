# 1.12.0 发行说明

完成 `kerlico` 与 `mihomo` 共同支持的 17 类代理协议、70 种传输与加密组合在公网 1Gbps 跨机（30s 固定时长）与内网环回（256MiB）双场景下的全量性能对标与数据面加速，70 个组合在两个场景下 100% 达到 `>= 78%` `mihomo` 吞吐标准（`LOW = 0`，`ERR = 0`）。

## 性能对标与数据面优化

- **17 协议 70 组合双场景 100% 达标**：
  - **公网 1Gbps 跨机 30s 固定时长（`perf-01` `<->` `perf-03`）**：70/70 组合达标，8 流并发吞吐比最低 `80.9%`、中位数 `104.4%`、均值 `124.2%`；70 组合累计 8 流总吞吐 `2,146.49 MiB/s`（`mihomo` 为 `1,967.28 MiB/s`，达 `109.1%`）；双端峰值常驻内存（`Peak RSS`）中位数 `62.0 MiB`（`mihomo` 为 `110.9 MiB`，节省 `44.9%`），单位流量 CPU 开销中位数 `19.58 CPU-ms/MiB`（`mihomo` 为 `23.77 CPU-ms/MiB`）。
  - **内网环回 256MiB（`perf-01 Loopback`）**：70/70 组合达标，8 流并发吞吐比最低 `80.5%`、中位数 `100.0%`、均值 `106.6%`；70 组合累计 8 流总吞吐 `47,555.28 MiB/s`（`mihomo` 为 `46,074.00 MiB/s`，达 `103.2%`）；双端峰值内存中位数 `61.5 MiB`（`mihomo` 为 `106.2 MiB`）。
- **通用转发、TLS 与多路复用优化（`core-api`、`net`、`xray-transport`）**：双向转发缓冲区 `DEFAULT_RELAY_BUFFER_SIZE` 扩至 `256 KiB`；`sing-mux`（`smux`/`yamux`/`h2mux`）引入 `64 KiB` `BufReader`、`48 KiB` 写帧聚批与 `poll_reserve` 背压许可，并及时回收已关闭入站流句柄；WebSocket、HTTPUpgrade 与 XHTTP/H2 载体消除逐帧零填充分配。
- **AEAD 流协议零清零读取与满帧写出（`vmess`、`vless`、`trojan`、`shadowsocks`、`ss2022`、`shadowsocksr`、`snell`）**：使用 `ReadBuf` 未初始化切片直接承接底层读取，消除用户态缓冲区重复清零；提升 `Shadowsocks`/`SS2022`（`0x3FFF`/`0xFFFF`）、`Snell`（`32 KiB`）与 `ShadowsocksR`（`tls1.2_ticket_auth` `64 KiB` 聚批）单帧载荷上限；优化 `VLESS` REALITY 与 XTLS Vision 连续缓冲读写路径。
- **QUIC 与自定义 UDP/TCP 传输加速（`hysteria1`、`hysteria2`、`tuic`、`trusttunnel`、`mieru`、`wireguard`）**：QUIC 与 UDP 协议统一配置 `4 MiB`–`8 MiB` 套接字收发缓冲与 `16 MiB`–`64 MiB` 流/连接接收窗口；`Mieru` 采用单向 `SimplexStream`、`64 KiB` 缓冲读写与无锁原子 UDP 序号；`WireGuard` 用户态 `smoltcp` 栈将 TCP 缓冲扩至 `2 MiB` 并支持单轮突发收发。
- **Sudoku 与 AnyTLS 深度重构（`sudoku`、`anytls`）**：`Sudoku` 预计算平坦化 `wire_clues` 与 `raw_hint_index`/`raw_group_index` 查找表、内联 `FastRng`、对 `32 KiB` 以上大块启用 `ParallelRecordStream` 双线程并行编解码，并在 HTTP 分离传输（`stream`/`poll`）中实现 `ReadBuf` 零拷贝直通与分档聚批（`stream` `64 KiB`、`poll` `32 KiB`）；`AnyTLS` 移除每流 `DuplexStream` 中转任务，改为 `PollSender<QueuedFrame>` 直写、多帧合并读取与 `128 KiB` TLS 读缓冲。

## English

Completes full-stack data-plane optimizations and benchmark verification across all 17 shared protocols and 70 transport/cipher combinations against `mihomo` (`v1.19.33`), achieving a 100% pass rate (`70/70 >= 78%` `mihomo` throughput, `LOW = 0`, `ERR = 0`) on both Public 1Gbps WAN (30s fixed duration) and Internal Loopback (256MiB).

- **100% pass rate across 17 protocols / 70 combinations.** On the Public 1Gbps WAN 30s benchmark (`perf-01` `<->` `perf-03`), `kerlico` achieves a median 8-stream throughput ratio of `104.4%` (mean `124.2%`, min `80.9%`, max `377.3%`), higher aggregate 8-stream throughput (`2,146.49 MiB/s` vs `1,967.28 MiB/s`, `109.1%`), `44.9%` lower median combined Peak RSS (`62.0 MiB` vs `110.9 MiB`), and `17.6%` lower median CPU cost (`19.58` vs `23.77 CPU-ms/MiB`). On the Internal Loopback 256MiB benchmark (`perf-01`), `kerlico` achieves a median 8-stream throughput ratio of `100.0%` (mean `106.6%`, min `80.5%`, aggregate `47,555.28 MiB/s` vs `46,074.00 MiB/s`).
- **Core relay, TLS, and multiplexing (`core-api`, `net`, `xray-transport`).** Expanded `DEFAULT_RELAY_BUFFER_SIZE` to `256 KiB`; added `64 KiB` `BufReader`, `48 KiB` write batching, `poll_reserve` backpressure, and inbound stream map cleanup in `sing-mux` (`smux`/`yamux`/`h2mux`); removed redundant zero-initialized allocations in WebSocket, HTTPUpgrade, and XHTTP/H2 carriers.
- **AEAD stream protocols (`vmess`, `vless`, `trojan`, `shadowsocks`, `ss2022`, `shadowsocksr`, `snell`).** Eliminated per-frame zero-filling via uninitialized `ReadBuf` slices; raised frame payload limits for `Shadowsocks`, `SS2022`, `Snell` (`32 KiB`), and `ShadowsocksR` (`64 KiB` `tls1.2_ticket_auth` batching); streamlined `VLESS` REALITY and XTLS Vision record processing.
- **QUIC and custom UDP/TCP transports (`hysteria1`, `hysteria2`, `tuic`, `trusttunnel`, `mieru`, `wireguard`).** Increased UDP socket buffers to `4 MiB`–`8 MiB` and QUIC stream/connection receive windows to `16 MiB`–`64 MiB`; replaced `DuplexStream` with `SimplexStream`, `64 KiB` buffered I/O, and atomic UDP sequence numbers in `Mieru`; expanded `WireGuard` userspace `smoltcp` TCP buffers to `2 MiB` with burst polling.
- **Sudoku and AnyTLS (`sudoku`, `anytls`).** Precomputed flat `wire_clues` and `raw_hint_index`/`raw_group_index` lookup tables, inlined `FastRng`, added `ParallelRecordStream` dual-thread record encoding/decoding for `>= 32 KiB` chunks, and enabled zero-copy `ReadBuf` pass-through plus mode-aware batching (`64 KiB` stream / `32 KiB` poll) for `Sudoku` HTTP split transports; replaced per-stream `DuplexStream` relay tasks in `AnyTLS` with direct `PollSender<QueuedFrame>`, multi-frame read coalescing, and a `128 KiB` buffered TLS reader.
